# The three sisters: personality and party roles

Decided Oct 2026. Appearance and weapons are in each sister's own character doc.

## Seffira (eldest, paladin)
- Leader of the trio. Calm, composed, cold-headed.
- Only weakness: Savon, her love interest. He always makes her lose her temper, and he is the only one who can.
- In battle: front line and tank.
- Holds to a code; won't do the dirty work.

## Viskan (middle, rogue)
- The cunning one.
- Usually the scout; in fights she flanks.
- Can shred whole groups with her dual chains when given an opening.
- Does the dirty work Seffira won't. This balance is a big part of why the trio is so strong and efficient.

## Lina (youngest, mage)
- Magic prodigy. Shy, rarely talks, hides behind her sisters.
- Magic: fire, ice, thunder; gravity manipulation; golem summoning; healing.
- Signature spells: meteor shower; a beam she can sustain for about 10 seconds that can literally raze a town.
- In battle: backline, either damage or healing.
