## The Church of the Black Sun
- Doctrine: darkness came before the First Word; light is a wound; the Long Night will unwrite the world.
- Five dark sacraments = the Dark combinations: Weighing 重, Cup 毒, Pyre 冥, Dream 魘, Judgement 劫 (each needs a partner caster). Lina could do all alone: they want her.
- Can write a dark word over a beast-mark (Hollow Stag, Skyhammer).
- Structure: Black Hierophant Severin Noct; Eclipses (Mother Sabine Dusk, abbess of the House of Dawn in Aurenhall, secretly); the Veiled (assassin pairs); the Shadowed (lay members). Initiate: Tomas Wick, in his Night Year.
- Recruits from Dark-casters persecuted by the Sun Church. Rivals: Children of the Void.
