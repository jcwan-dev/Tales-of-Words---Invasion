## The Hollow Hand's art: gate-sealing (點穴 diǎnxué)
Motto: "A word needs a hand, a breath and a voice. Take one."
Seven qi gates: two wrist gates (no clean strokes), throat (no naming), breath (no shine), heart (panic, understanding slips), spine (cuts body and breath), crown (forbidden: can sever the gift or kill). Clean strike = minutes; master strike = hours or days. Healing at Master depth can reopen gates. Tools: gate-irons (wrist cuffs), needles, smoke. Ranks: Open Palm (leader), Palms, Fingers, Knuckles (7-year apprentices). Many hunters are Silent-born.

## The Lampless
Rules: pay the cellar; no work in another's city without leave; never rob the temples of the poor; any member on the run gets three nights' shelter. Ranks: Keyholders, Knives, Keys, Mice, Runners. Sign: a snuffed candle.
