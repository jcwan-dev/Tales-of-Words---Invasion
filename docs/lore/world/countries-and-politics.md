# Countries and politics (placeholder layout)

Created Oct 2026, regions and map added 12 Oct 2026. Full entries for every country, region, city, sea, government and character are in the wiki (Tales of Words Codex). All names there are variables: renaming an entry in the wiki updates every mention, including the labels on the map.

## Map layout
The wiki's map entry, "The Known World", is an ink charter in the style of old sea charts. Place names are drawn live on top of the image.
- **Merathis**: the main continent. Calvessa (west), Aurendal (centre, largest), Valdreth (east, runs off the map's east edge).
- **Elvaren**: island north-west of the continent, across the **Grey Strait** (scene of the Strait War).
- **Zerath**: volcanic island south-east of the continent, across the **Ember Sea**.
- **Western Ocean**: west of everything; nobody has crossed it. **Sea of Mists**: the foggy northern sea.
- **Sea lanes:** Port Liraine to Saltcrown (north), Port Liraine to Embermouth (south, around the Amber Coast), Saltcrown to Wardhaven (across the strait).
- **Great river:** rises in western Valdreth, flows west past Goldmere and Aurenhall, and reaches the sea in the Sallow Delta at Port Liraine.

## Regions and cities
- **Elvaren:** Fjordreach (wild north and west fjords; towns Norrhavn, Hvalsund), Silverhills (silver mines; Graylock Keep, seat of Eamon Graylock), Gullcoast (royal south-east coast; Saltcrown).
- **Zerath:** Ashspine (volcanic ridge; Ilyrion), Spice Terraces (fertile west, worked by the Silent; Embermouth harbour, Tessar), Obsidian Coast (black-sand east; Cinderhold, Ink Guard fortress).
- **Calvessa:** Sallow Delta (Port Liraine), Vinehills (wine country on the border; Belmora, Mirenza the river-toll town), Amber Coast (southern shipyards; Torrecina, Duquay fleets).
- **Aurendal:** Northern Marches (Hadrian's frontier on the strait; Wardhaven), Heartlands (river plains, Mirrormere lake; Aurenhall, Goldmere), Spine Mountains (iron; Ironford), Eastern Marches (border with Valdreth; Eastwatch, held by the Gilded Company).
- **Valdreth** (one region per great house): Ravenmark (Corvane; Morvane), Ironvale Reach (Ironvale mines; Kharn), Thornsteppe (Thorne horse plateau; Thornkeep), Deepwood (Sablewood forest; Sablehall), Ashgrave Peaks (Fallen Empire ruins; Ashgrave Hold).

## Main political tensions
- **Aurendal succession:** the old king has never named an heir. Crown Prince Hadrian (army, Church) vs Prince Cassian (his mother Queen Liesel, Calvessan money).
- **Forbidden romance:** Princess Maelis of Elvaren and Prince Cassian of Aurendal have written in secret for three years.
- **The great debt:** Aurendal owes Calvessa a debt that can never be repaid (discovered by the auditor Tobiah Quill). Goldmere's royal granaries are secretly empty.
- **Elvaren's male-line plot:** the queen's cousin Eamon Graylock wants the crown, secretly funded by Celestine Duquay of Calvessa.
- **War in the east:** Aleksei Thorne of Valdreth wants to invade Aurendal when the king dies and has a secret pact with Oren Kail of Zerath (battle-mages and golems for a coastal city). The Gilded Company's contract at Eastwatch ends soon.
- **The Silent of Zerath:** non-mages have no vote; Kesh leads a growing movement, secretly in contact with councillor Ilune Tessaly.
- **Magic and the Church:** Aurendal's Sun Church wants magic banned; the royal Mage-Warden Elias Crane secretly hides child mages.
- **Golems and miners:** Zerathi golems are replacing Valdreth's miners at Kharn; one golem is digging toward a sealed vault of the Fallen Empire.

## Magic (consistent with world/magic-system.md)
Zerath's council seats are the seven base words plus Order and Healing, in traditional characters: Order 理 Lǐ, Fire 火 Huǒ, Healing 愈 Yù, Earth 土 Tǔ (Davor Grael, a stone specialist), Thunder 雷 Léi, Dark 暗 Àn, Water 水 Shuǐ, Wind 風 Fēng, Light 光 Guāng. Other words used: Ward 盾 Dùn, Seal 鎖 Suǒ. Glyph-carvers can carve words into objects to hold their power.
