# Magic system: words of power

**Status:** Core idea only (Oct 2026). To be developed in the wiki.

## Core idea
Magic in the Tales of Words universe is based on words. A caster **summons a word and its power**.

The image that defines it: a mage holds an open hand in front of them and shouts the word, for example "Huo!". The written character for that word (火, fire) appears in front of them, and its power takes effect as a fire attack.

## Known so far
- Casting = speaking the word aloud + the written character manifesting + the word's power acting.
- Example given: Huo / 火 / fire, shown as a Chinese character.

## Open questions for the wiki
- Which script or scripts words use: Chinese characters only, or several writing systems in different cultures.
- Who can summon words, and how words are learned (study, bloodline, teachers, inscriptions).
- Whether words can be combined into compounds, and what that costs.
- Limits and costs of casting (exhaustion, voice, focus, danger of misspeaking).
- How Lina's magic (fire, ice, thunder, gravity, golems, healing, meteor shower, the sustained beam) works in terms of words.
- Whether words can be carved or written into objects to hold their power.
