# Bestiary

Status: placeholder (Oct 2026). Lives in the wiki's Bestiary chapter, introduced by `{{bestiary-book}}` (the Black Pine Lodge's Bestiary Book). Every creature is a name variable.

## Rules
- Myths are real: Asian chimeras (qilin, pixiu, baku, nue, raiju, xiezhi, guardian lions) are species; a few individuals are named legends.
- Danger scale: 1 Nuisance · 2 Dangerous · 3 Deadly · 4 Calamity · 5 Legend.
- Beast-marks: a word grown into the body (one, rarely two). Marked entries carry a `Glyph` fact-box row, which shows as the picture and list thumbnail until an image is added.
- Two marks: Fenghuang (火+光 fuse into 曜); the Widow of the West grew 濤 in grief. Changed mark: Skyhammer (雷 → 劫). An imugi that grows a second mark becomes a dragon.
- Unknown words (no human can cast): 龍 (dragons), 夢 (baku), 言 (White Sage / bai ze), 化 (Kun), 不 (Bulgasari), 年 (the Nian); the Taotie's mark is unreadable.
- Renderer: "the {{id}}" before a title starting with "The" now folds into one article automatically.

## Named
- **Bulgasari** (`{{bulgasari}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 不): The iron-eater of the {{silverhills}}: a beast that cannot be killed, which grows by eating metal. Its mark is 不, 'not'.
- **Greymother** (`{{greymother}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 捷): The old she-wolf who leads the largest pack on the {{thornsteppe}}, and has followed the {{wolfclaw}} gauntlets for three years.
- **Kun** (`{{kun}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 化): A fish so large no one has seen all of it, in the far {{western-ocean}}. Every thousand years it becomes a bird, Peng, and flies.
- **Mercy** (`{{mercy-qilin}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 愈): The qilin that walked into the throne hall of {{aurenhall}} at {{aldric}}'s coronation, fifty years ago. The court waits to see whom it chooses next.
- **Skyhammer** (`{{skyhammer}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 劫): A storm eagle of the {{ashgrave-peaks}} so old its mark has changed: it throws black lightning that hunts its target.
- **The Crow Abbot** (`{{crow-abbot}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 風): The great tengu of the {{ashgrave-peaks}}, who taught the first masters of the {{drawn-blade}} and still tests anyone who climbs his mountain.
- **The Ember King** (`{{ember-king}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 龍): The Dragon King of the {{ember-sea}}: a red long dragon older than {{zerath}}'s council, to whom the island's fishers still make offerings.
- **The Glutton Below** (`{{taotie}}`, 5 · Legend): A taotie, the insatiable devourer of imperial legend, sealed beneath {{ashgrave-hold}}. Its mark is the one no carver can read.
- **The Grey Maw** (`{{grey-maw}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 重): The kraken of the {{sea-of-mists}}, which rises in fog every few years and takes a whole fleet.
- **The Hollow Stag** (`{{hollow-stag}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 暗): Something vast and antlered walking in the heart of the {{deepwood}}, driving every marked beast out of the forest.
- **The Lady of Nine Tails** (`{{lady-nine-tails}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 幻): A nine-tailed fox spirit who has lived among the merchant princes of {{port-liraine}} for three centuries, under a dozen names.
- **The Nian** (`{{the-nian}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 年): The beast that comes down from the mountains on the last night of every year. It fears red, fire and noise, and its mark is 年, 'year'.
- **The White Sage** (`{{white-sage}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 言): The bai ze: a white, speaking beast with many eyes that dictated the first bestiary to the emperor, and is said to wander still.
- **The Widow of the West** (`{{widow-of-the-west}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 濤): The great sea serpent whose mate was killed by {{ysolde}}'s grandmother. She still hunts the {{western-ocean}} for the armour made from him.

## Dragon
- **Ember Drake** (`{{ember-drake}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 火): Four-legged winged drakes of the volcanic ridges, marked 火 on the throat. The most hunted marked beast in the world.
- **Imugi** (`{{imugi}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 水): Great sea and lake serpents that strive for a thousand years to become dragons. Marked with Water; the one that grows a second mark ascends.
- **Jiao** (`{{jiao-flood-dragon}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 泥): Hornless flood dragons of the great rivers: lesser cousins of the long dragons, marked with Mire, who bring the floods that drown the delta.
- **Long Dragon** (`{{long-dragon}}`, 5 · Legend — mark 龍): The great serpentine dragons of rain and sea: wingless, whiskered, older than kingdoms. Every one carries 龍, a word no human has ever made shine.
- **Wyvern** (`{{wyvern}}`, 3 · Deadly): Two-legged winged hunters of the high plateaus, with a venomous tail sting. Unmarked, but deadly enough without one.

## Chimera
- **Baku** (`{{baku}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 夢): Tapir-like chimeras that eat nightmares. Their mark, 夢, is a word no human has ever cast.
- **Chimera** (`{{chimera}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 炎): Three-headed volcanic beasts: lion, goat and serpent. The goat head carries the Inferno mark.
- **Dragon Horse** (`{{dragon-horse}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 風): Scaled, maned horses with dragon blood, swift as wind. Some carry the Wind mark. House Thorne's riders would kill for one.
- **Fenghuang** (`{{fenghuang}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 曜): The phoenix pairs of the far mountains: the only known creature with two marks, Fire and Light, which fuse into Sunfire when they sing.
- **Griffin** (`{{griffin}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 風): Eagle-headed, lion-bodied guardians of mountain gold. Some carry the Wind mark. Tamed griffins are the rarest mounts in the world.
- **Guardian Lion** (`{{guardian-lion}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 盾): Curly-maned lion-dogs bred by monasteries for a thousand years to guard temples and palaces. Some are born marked with Ward.
- **Manticore** (`{{manticore}}`, 3 · Deadly): Red lion-beasts with a near-human face, three rows of teeth and a tail that fires venomous spines.
- **Nue** (`{{nue}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 毒): Ill-omened night chimeras with a monkey's face, a badger's body, tiger's legs and a snake for a tail. Marked with Poison; their cry brings sickness.
- **Pixiu** (`{{pixiu}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 土): Winged lion-dragons that eat gold, silver and ore. Marked with Earth. Merchants keep their statues for luck; miners dread the real thing.
- **Qilin** (`{{qilin}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 愈): The gentle, scaled, hoofed beasts of good omen, marked with Healing. A qilin appears when a just ruler is born or crowned.
- **Raiju** (`{{raiju}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 雷): Thunder beasts: blue-white wolf-cats made of fur and lightning, marked with Thunder, that fall to earth with the bolt.
- **Xiezhi** (`{{xiezhi}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 理): One-horned goat-lions that butt the guilty. Marked with Order. The courts of {{aurendal}} keep one in every high hall of justice.

## Spirit
- **Fox Spirit** (`{{fox-spirit}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 幻): Foxes that grow a tail every century and a mind with each tail. At nine tails they take human shape and the mark of Mirage.
- **Kappa** (`{{kappa}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 水): River imps with a water-filled dish on their heads that holds their mark. Bow to a kappa and it will bow back, and spill its power.
- **Keening Spirit** (`{{keening-spirit}}`, 1 · Nuisance): Grey women of the Elvaren shores whose wail foretells a death at sea. They are called the Drowned Mother's daughters.
- **Nekomata** (`{{nekomata}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 冥): Old cats whose tails have split in two. Marked with Ghostfire, they can raise the dead like puppets.
- **Ningyo** (`{{ningyo}}`, 2 · Dangerous): Fish with a human face whose flesh grants a long life. The {{amari}} say their own long lives began with one.
- **Snow Woman** (`{{snow-woman}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 冰): Pale spirits of blizzards, marked with Ice. They freeze travellers with a breath, and spare the ones who keep their secret.
- **Sun Crow** (`{{sun-crow}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 光): Three-legged crows of golden light, marked with Light. Sacred to [[The Sun Church]], they nest only where the sun rises first.
- **Tengu** (`{{tengu}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 風): Crow-winged mountain spirits who live as hermit swordsmen. Marked with Wind. Legend says they taught the first masters of the Drawn Blade.
- **Tokkaebi** (`{{tokkaebi}}`, 2 · Dangerous): Horned tricksters of the islands' hills, who carry magic clubs and love wrestling, gambling and fools.
- **Will-o'-Wisp** (`{{wisp}}`, 1 · Nuisance): Drifting lights of marsh and ruin, born where a cracked glyph spilled its power. They lead travellers astray.

- **Ghost Leopard** (`{{ghost-leopard}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 暗): Snow leopards of the high peaks, marked with Dark, invisible at night.

- **Ironhide Bear** (`{{ironhide-bear}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 力): Mountain bears with hide like boiled leather. The strongest are marked with Strength.
- **Lantern Oyster** (`{{lantern-oyster}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 光): Oysters of the obsidian reefs whose pearls glow with their Light mark.
- **Mire Crocodile** (`{{mire-crocodile}}`, 3 · Deadly): Huge armoured crocodiles of the delta swamps, old enough to remember the city being built.
- **Morvane Raven** (`{{morvane-raven}}`, 1 · Nuisance — mark 心): Huge, clever ravens of the Valdrethi highlands. They talk, they remember faces and House Corvane uses them as spies.

- **Steppe Wolf** (`{{steppe-wolf}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 捷): Big grey wolves of the high plateau, hunting in packs of twenty. Pack leaders are often marked with Agility.
- **Stone-Shell Turtle** (`{{stone-shell-turtle}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 土): Giant sea turtles whose shells grow into islands, marked with Earth. Sailors have landed on them by mistake.

- **Thorn-Boar** (`{{thorn-boar}}`, 2 · Dangerous — mark 堅): Huge boars of the pine forests, bristling with bony spines. Old males are marked with Endurance and can take a lance charge.

- **Basilisk** (`{{basilisk}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 堅): Crowned serpents whose gaze hardens flesh into stone. Marked with Endurance. Their small cousins, cockatrices, plague farms.
- **Frost Giant** (`{{frost-giant}}`, 4 · Calamity): Blue-skinned giants of the northern ice, three times a man's height.
- **Great Centipede** (`{{great-centipede}}`, 4 · Calamity): Mountain-sized centipedes that coil around peaks. Only saliva-dipped arrows pierce their shell, the legend says.
- **Harpy** (`{{harpy}}`, 2 · Dangerous): Vulture-winged women of the obsidian cliffs who swoop on ships and steal.
- **Hydra** (`{{hydra}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 愈): Many-headed swamp serpents marked with Healing: cut off one head and two grow back.
- **Inkling** (`{{inkling}}`, 1 · Nuisance): Small blobs of living ink born from cracked glyphs, which crawl through libraries eating paper.
- **Jorōgumo** (`{{jorogumo}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 魘): Spider-women who weave in abandoned houses and lure travellers with a beautiful face. Marked with Dread.
- **Kraken** (`{{kraken}}`, 4 · Calamity): Island-sized cephalopods of the cold seas, rising from the deep to drag ships down.
- **Minotaur** (`{{minotaur}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 力): Bull-headed giants of the imperial labyrinths. Some carry the Strength mark.
- **Oni** (`{{oni}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 力): Horned ogres of the Valdrethi highlands with iron clubs and terrible appetites. Many carry the Strength mark.
- **Sea Serpent** (`{{sea-serpent}}`, 4 · Calamity — mark 水): The great serpents of the open ocean, marked with Water along the spine. They sink ships and are hunted for their scales.
- **Siren** (`{{siren}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 心): Fog-singers of the strait whose song reaches into the mind. Marked with Focus, turned to the hunt.
- **Troll** (`{{troll}}`, 3 · Deadly — mark 土): Hulking cave-dwellers of the Elvaren hills, marked with Earth: in sunlight, they turn to stone.

## Undead
- **Draugr** (`{{draugr}}`, 3 · Deadly): The drowned dead of the Haldar, risen from the sea with seaweed in their beards and grudges in their hearts.
- **Hungry Ghost** (`{{hungry-ghost}}`, 1 · Nuisance): Spirits of the neglected dead: huge bellies, needle-thin throats, never satisfied.
- **Jiangshi** (`{{jiangshi}}`, 2 · Dangerous): Stiff hopping corpses in old court robes that drain the breath of the living. Sealed with paper glyph talismans.

## Construct
- **Golem** (`{{golem}}`, 2 · Dangerous): Stone figures moved by the Animation glyph. Some are built today; the old ones still walk.
- **Imperial Sentinel** (`{{imperial-sentinel}}`, 3 · Deadly): Armoured guardians of [[The Fallen Empire]]'s ruins, carved with glyphs no one can carve anymore.
