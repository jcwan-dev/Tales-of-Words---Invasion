# Viskan

**Role:** Rogue (dual chains)
**Status:** Concept (Oct 2026). Sister of Seffira and Lina.

## Appearance
- **Height / build:** Tallest of the three sisters, about 1.80 m (a man's height); slender and long-limbed.
- **Hair:** Silver.
- **Eyes:** Pale icy blue, same as her sisters.
- Considered a great beauty by the world's standards.

## Weapons
- **Dual chains:** Two long weighted chains, one per hand, in the style of Vella's dual chains in Vindictus. Each ends in a short blade or hooked weight; kept partly wrapped around the forearms. Used for long whipping arcs, wrapping and pulling targets, and a constant spinning, dance-like rhythm.
