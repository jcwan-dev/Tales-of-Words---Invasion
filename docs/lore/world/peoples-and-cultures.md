# Peoples & Cultures

Status: placeholder (Oct 2026). Every name is a wiki variable: rename the entry and every `{{id}}` reference updates. Glyphs use traditional characters only.

## Peoples

- **The Haldar** (`{{haldar}}`, world): The tall seafaring people of {{elvaren}}: the tallest folk of the known world, pale-skinned and long-limbed, shaped by cold seas and long winters. _Homeland: {{elvaren}}; Culture: {{tide-custom}}_
- **The Amari** (`{{amari}}`, world): The warm-skinned island people of {{zerath}}, sturdy and famously long-lived, shaped by volcanic soil, sea trade and a magocracy that divides them into mages and the Silent. _Homeland: {{zerath}}; Culture: {{island-way}}_
- **The Mereth** (`{{mereth}}`, world): The people of the continent, a blend of two founding strands mixed for a thousand years.
  - **The Vael** (`{{vael}}`): European-looking; crossed the north-western sea in the Fallen Empire's last centuries (distant kin of the Haldar). Source of knighthood, heraldry, plate armour and most names.
  - **The Sennai** (`{{sennai}}`): East Asian; native, builders of the Fallen Empire, the Old Script, the Old Readings and the martial arts.
  - Rough shares: Calvessa 35% Vael / 45% mixed / 20% Sennai; Aurendal 50/35/15; Valdreth 30/35/35. _Homeland: {{merathis}}: {{calvessa}}, {{aurendal}}, {{valdreth}}_

## Cultures

- **Tide Custom** (`{{tide-custom}}`, elvaren): The culture of {{elvaren}}: sea-faith, crew loyalty, long coats and longer winters. Its fighting arts make use of the {{haldar}}'s great reach. _Inspired by: Korea and the northern seas_
- **The Island Way** (`{{island-way}}`, zerath): The culture of {{zerath}}: volcano-reverence, ancestor tombs, bright dyed cloth and a society split between mages and the Silent. Inspired by the Ryukyu islands of Okinawa. _Inspired by: The Ryukyu Kingdom (Okinawa)_
- **Canal Custom** (`{{canal-custom}}`, calvessa): The culture of {{calvessa}}: southern, mercantile, crowded and loud, built on boats, banks and bargaining. Inspired by the trading ports of southern China. _Inspired by: Southern China: Canton, Hong Kong, the canal towns_
- **The Old Rites** (`{{old-rites}}`, aurendal): The culture of {{aurendal}}: imperial ceremony, filial duty, rice and wheat fields, and the monasteries of [[The Sun Church]]. Inspired by imperial China. _Inspired by: Imperial China_
- **The Highland Code** (`{{highland-code}}`, valdreth): The culture of {{valdreth}}: five warrior houses, a strict code of honour, ancestor crypts, snow and steel. Inspired by feudal Japan. _Inspired by: Feudal Japan_

## Languages

- **Tongues and Scripts** (`{{tongues-and-scripts}}`, world): The spoken languages of the known world, and the one script they share: the old characters in which every word of power is written.

## Martial arts

- **Wave-Kick** (`{{wave-kick}}`, elvaren): The kicking art of {{elvaren}}: flowing, dance-like footwork learned on rolling decks, using the {{haldar}}'s long legs to strike from beyond arm's reach. _Culture: {{tide-custom}}; Type: Empty hand: kicking art; Inspired by: Korean taekkyeon_
- **Long Reach** (`{{long-reach}}`, elvaren): The weapon art of {{elvaren}}: the two-handed long sabre, the boarding spear and the harpoon, wielded with the reach of the {{haldar}}. _Culture: {{tide-custom}}; Type: Weapon art: long blades and spears; Inspired by: Korean and Chinese long-weapon arts (the long sabre, *woldo*); Weapons: Long sabre, boarding spear, harpoon, boarding axe_
- **Island Hand** (`{{island-hand}}`, zerath): The empty-hand art of {{zerath}}'s Silent, created when they were forbidden to carry blades: hardened fists, crushing strikes and patterns practised ten thousand times. _Culture: {{island-way}}; Type: Empty hand: striking art; Inspired by: Okinawan karate (te)_
- **Field Tools** (`{{field-tools}}`, zerath): The weapon art of {{zerath}}'s Silent: staffs, sickles, flails, rice-mill handles and boat oars, turned into weapons because blades were forbidden. _Culture: {{island-way}}; Type: Weapon art: farm and boat tools; Inspired by: Okinawan kobudō; Weapons: Staff, sickles, flail, three-pronged truncheons, mill-handle batons, boat oar_
- **Canal Fist** (`{{canal-fist}}`, calvessa): The close-range fighting art of {{calvessa}}: tight guards, chain punches and sticking hands, born on canal boats and opera stages where there is no room to swing. _Culture: {{canal-custom}}; Type: Empty hand: close-range art; Inspired by: Southern Chinese kung fu (Wing Chun)_
- **Butterfly and Pole** (`{{butterfly-and-pole}}`, calvessa): The weapon art of {{calvessa}}: paired butterfly swords for close quarters and the boatman's long pole for keeping enemies at bay. _Culture: {{canal-custom}}; Type: Weapon art: twin short blades and long pole; Inspired by: Wing Chun butterfly swords and the long pole; Weapons: Butterfly swords, long pole, rapier (modern duelling)_
- **Sun Fist** (`{{sun-fist}}`, aurendal): The monastic fighting art of {{aurendal}}'s Sun Church: powerful stances, animal forms and iron-body training, taught to monks and the faithful. _Culture: {{old-rites}}; Type: Empty hand: monastic hard style; Inspired by: Shaolin kung fu_
- **The Hundred Weapons** (`{{hundred-weapons}}`, aurendal): The weapon arts of {{aurendal}}'s army and nobility: the spear, king of weapons; the straight sword, gentleman of weapons; the sabre, the glaive and the staff. _Culture: {{old-rites}}; Type: Weapon art: imperial army arts; Inspired by: Chinese weapon arts (spear, jian, dao, guandao); Weapons: Spear, straight sword, sabre, glaive, staff, bow_
- **The Drawn Blade** (`{{drawn-blade}}`, valdreth): The sword art of {{valdreth}}'s warrior houses: the single perfect stroke drawn from the scabbard, settling a duel before the other blade is free. _Culture: {{highland-code}}; Type: Weapon art: sword-drawing; Inspired by: Japanese iaijutsu and kenjutsu; Weapons: Long sword and short sword (worn together)_
- **Horse and Bow** (`{{horse-and-bow}}`, valdreth): The mounted arts of {{valdreth}}: archery from a galloping horse, the long glaive of the riders and of house women, and the hunting bow of the deep forests. _Culture: {{highland-code}}; Type: Weapon art: mounted archery and glaive; Inspired by: Japanese yabusame, kyūdō and naginata; Weapons: Asymmetric longbow, glaive, lance, hunting bow_
- **Gate-Sealing** (`{{gate-sealing}}`, calvessa): The secret art of the {{hollow-hand}}: precise strikes to the seven qi gates of the body that stop a caster's breath from reaching a glyph. Descended from {{island-hand}} and {{canal-fist}}. _Culture: {{canal-custom}}; Type: Empty hand: pressure-point art against mages; Inspired by: Chinese diǎnxué (pressure-point striking) and dim mak legends_
