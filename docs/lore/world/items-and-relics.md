# Items and relics (placeholder names)

Created 12 Oct 2026. Full pages in the wiki (Items & Relics), with a "Relic Index" page listing everything. Names are variables there. All glyphs are traditional Chinese.

## Rules decided
- **Relic tiers:** Imperial relic (Fallen Empire, lost words, works for anyone), Mark-forged (beast-mark set into the object, steady power for anyone), Master-forged (no magic, legendary craft), Holy relic (blessed; some secretly hide a carved word), Carved (glyph charged by a mage).
- **Carved items are Passive or Active.** Passive = one steady effect for anyone (lamp burns, lock holds). Active = released on command, needs a caster's breath; useless to non-casters, except Bram Holloway's secret carvings.
- **Carving quality:** Hand-carved (fades, needs recharging), Mark-carved (from a beast-mark, lasts far longer), Imperial carving (never fades, cannot be copied).
- Two glyphs carved together give their combination's effect (e.g. 水+風 = Ice for the Coldbox).

## The sisters' gear
- **Seffira:** Vesperis (longsword; weightless to an honest hand, leaden to an oathbreaker), The Gilded Wyrm (heater shield; fire-mark under the enamel, crest wakes when she holds her ground), The Violet Vigil (her armour).
- **Viskan:** Hush and Lash (imperial chain pair; links lengthen to ~12 m at will; Hush is silent, Lash rings near blood).
- **Lina:** Brushwillow (white willow staff with a brush-like tassel; carved 土 lets it stand alone so both hands are free; imperial 氣 carving stores breath, which is how she holds Sunlance ~10 s).
Origins of all four are left open.

## Notable plot items
- **Dawnbringer:** whoever draws it next may decide Aurendal's succession.
- **The Silence Needle:** could seal a caster's gift for ever without touching the crown gate.
- **The Last Arrow:** seventh of seven, cannot miss; every history of the empire's fall mentions it.
- **The Void Shard (虛):** fragment of the cast that ended the Fallen Empire; why Sethra Vaul sealed the Scriptorium's hall.
- **The Breath Gourd (氣):** the only other breath carving besides Brushwillow.
- **The Hollow Key:** opens any imperial door; Tasha Volk's mother may have carried it.
- Holy relics of the Sun Church (Hierarch's Staff, Sun Medallion, Whiteguard Plate) secretly work through carved Light glyphs, which the Church condemns.
- The Ember Charm (火) from the game spec is in the wiki as a common Active charm.
